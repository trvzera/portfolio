Giovanni Trivellato / About me

Code, design
and motion.

I’m Giovanni Trivellato, 18 years old, from Belo Horizonte, Brazil. I create digital experiences that bring web development, design, and motion together.

Technology and design have interested me since childhood. Today, I turn that interest into pages, animations, and projects that work well and communicate clearly.

01

About me

My path connects two areas I enjoy: development and visual creation. On the web, I organize content and build interfaces. In video, I work with rhythm, motion, and storytelling.

I want to keep learning in both areas and use that experience in increasingly complete projects.

02

Education

I’m in my third year of technical high school at COTEMIG, studying Web and Mobile Development. Beyond school, I take online courses to expand my knowledge and experiment with new tools.

I plan to pursue iOS development, designing and building apps from the first screen to the final code.

03

Development

I have experience with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for building web pages and interfaces. I also currently study and use Python, Swift, and SwiftUI.

My goal is to work across the whole app process, from interface design to programming. I also maintain trvzera.com.br, where I curate recommendations for setups, RPG, books, and manga.

04

Motion & design

I edit short and long videos, clips, motion pieces, and animations. I use After Effects, Photoshop, and Blender to explore movement, imagery, and composition.

I create intros, website animations, MMVs, and 3D edits. These projects are collected in my Behance portfolio ↗.

That visual experience shapes how I think about transitions, hierarchy, and details in interfaces.
